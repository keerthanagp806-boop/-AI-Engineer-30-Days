# Day 1 — Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Deep Learning

## Artificial Intelligence (AI)
AI is the broad field of creating computer systems that perform tasks that usually require human-like intelligence, such as understanding language, recognising images, planning, and making decisions.

**Example:** A navigation app recommending a route based on traffic.

## Machine Learning (ML)
ML is a subset of AI. A system learns patterns from data so it can make predictions or decisions rather than relying only on manually written rules.

**Example:** An email spam filter learns from examples of spam and non-spam messages.

## Deep Learning (DL)
DL is a subset of ML that uses neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns. It is often used for images, audio, and language.

**Example:** A model recognising a dog in a photograph.

## Relationship

```text
Artificial Intelligence
└── Machine Learning
    └── Deep Learning
```

## Remember
- AI is the broadest field.
- ML is one approach within AI.
- DL is one approach within ML.
- Not every AI system uses ML, and not every ML system uses DL.
