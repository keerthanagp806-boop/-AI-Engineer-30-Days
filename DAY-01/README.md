# Day 01 — AI Engineer and AI Foundations

## Topics covered
1. What an AI Engineer is.
2. Roles and responsibilities of an AI Engineer.
3. AI Engineer vs. Machine Learning Engineer.
4. AI vs. AGI.
5. The impact of AI on product development.

## Key takeaways
- An AI Engineer builds applications and systems that use AI models.
- Typical work includes selecting models, integrating APIs, preparing data, building application workflows, testing outputs and considering safety, cost and reliability.
- An ML Engineer often focuses more deeply on developing, training, evaluating and deploying machine-learning systems. The roles can overlap.
- AI refers broadly to systems that perform tasks associated with intelligence.
- AGI is a proposed/general concept of AI with broad, human-like ability across many tasks; today's narrow AI systems should not be described as AGI.
- AI can add features such as chat, recommendations, summarization and document understanding to products.
